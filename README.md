# hexaworld

A survival game on a wrapping hexagonal world: an island ecosystem with seasons, plants and animals that live on
their own, and a character who gathers, crafts, builds and survives.

## Play

Play online at **https://bytecollage.github.io/hexaworld/**, or open `index.html` in a browser (double-click).
There is no server, build step or install.
